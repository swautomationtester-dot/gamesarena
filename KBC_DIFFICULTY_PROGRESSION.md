# GamesArena KBC Difficulty Progression

Every 5-question KBC game now follows this requested pattern:

- **Q1 — ₹10: Easy** warm-up. Difficulty 1 is preferred.
- **Q2 — ₹20: Easy**. Another easy question; difficulty 1 is preferred, with difficulty 2 as fallback.
- **Q3 — ₹30: Tough**. Only questions with source difficulty 3 or higher are eligible.
- **Q4 — ₹40: Hard/Tougher**. Difficulty 4+ is preferred, with difficulty 3 only as a fallback if necessary.
- **Q5 — ₹50: Perficient Finale**. The fifth question is category-locked to **Perficient** and the hardest available Perficient question is preferred.

The displayed difficulty labels are Easy, Easy, Tough, Hard and Final — Perficient. Options are shuffled without changing the correct answer. Room-level used-question tracking prevents repeats until the bank is exhausted/reset.
