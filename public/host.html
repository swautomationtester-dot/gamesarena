GAMESARENA QUIZ

📖 Game Rules

  1. Only registered and payment-approved participants can play their selected quiz.
  2. Open registration, then use Fastest Finger to select the next contestant.
  3. Fastest Finger is a Sequence Challenge: arrange the displayed numbers from lowest to highest within 15 seconds. The first player to complete the sequence wins.
  4. The selected contestant plays 10 questions with four options each.
  5. Q1–Q4: 30 seconds. Q5–Q10: 60 seconds.
  6. Contestant answers are locked and require Host approval before reveal.
  7. Lifelines: 50:50, Audience Poll and Phone-a-Friend.
  8. Prize ladder: ₹5, ₹10, ₹15, ₹20, ₹25, ₹30, ₹35, ₹40, ₹45, ₹50.
  9. ₹20 Safe Money is secured after Q4 and is available from Q5. ₹40 Safe Money is secured after Q8 and is available from Q9. Safe Quit requires Host approval.
  10. Wrong answer or timeout eliminates the contestant from the current game.
  11. Q10 is the ₹50 final. Completing all 10 questions wins the maximum ₹50.
The Host can show or close these rules for everyone. Participants and TV can also close the panel locally.
