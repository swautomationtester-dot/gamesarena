# GamesArena Question Bank v2

The KBC quiz now uses a mixed Indian question bank with 180 total questions:

- 100 Kids General Knowledge (existing)
- 20 South Indian Cinema
- 20 Indian Cinema
- 20 Politics & Indian Polity
- 20 Technology

Each 5-question game deliberately covers one question from each theme, with difficulty increasing across the ladder. Question IDs are tracked per room so a question already used in that room is not selected again until the available bank for that theme is exhausted.

Answer options are shuffled on every game while the correct answer index is recalculated server-side.
